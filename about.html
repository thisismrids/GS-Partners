About

Two backgrounds built for this problem.

GS Partners pairs private-equity-style commercial diligence with credit and financial-structuring rigor, the same combination large firms use to vet a deal, aimed at a category gap instead.

Origin

Where the idea came from

The idea started at a conversation about vetting a group investment the way a private equity fund would, at a fraction of the scale. We realised the same diligence discipline applies just as well to a question most founders never get answered properly: where, precisely, is a competitor beating you, and what would it take to close it.
