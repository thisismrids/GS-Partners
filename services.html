Services

One service. Done properly.

We don't run a menu of offerings. We do competitive gap analysis for founders and promoters in fragmented, disrupted categories — and we do the free half first, so you can judge the work before you pay for it.

The model

Most advisory pitches ask you to trust a slide deck. We'd rather show you something true about your own business first.

01 — Research

Map the category

We identify your closest competitors and the channels where the category is actually moving right now — quick commerce, marketplaces, D2C, whichever applies. Every source is public and checkable by you.

02 — Reveal

One free finding

A one-page competitive gap snapshot: what we found, exactly where, and roughly how much demand it represents. Free — it's the proof of the work, not a teaser for it.

03 — Roadmap

Paid, if it's worth it

If the gap is worth closing, we scope an execution roadmap: what to do, in what order, and what it should reasonably cost — priced to the scope, not a standard retainer.

Who this is for

Your category doesn't have one dominant player yet — it's genuinely fragmented.

Something is actively shifting under you — a new channel, format, or entrant — not just a cosmetic complaint.

You, or someone who can decide, is directly reachable — not three layers of gatekeeping away.

Your business already has real revenue or funding on the line, so a fix is worth paying for.

A prioritized plan to close the gap, scoped and priced to your situation — with optional support seeing it through.
